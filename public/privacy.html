Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version

CORE MDM manages what a device can do. It does not read your messages, track your location, record your browsing history, or access your photos, contacts or files. It collects the minimum needed to show a phone in your console and apply the rules you set.

What we collect

From a managed device:

  • Device info: model, manufacturer, Android version, and a stable hardware identifier (IMEI/serial/Android ID, where available).
  • Status: online/offline and a "last seen" time (a heartbeat).
  • The policies and commands you apply to the device.
  • A list of installed apps (package name, label, version) so you can see and manage them. No app content is collected.

From an administrator (you): your sign-in identity (email and account ID) from Firebase Authentication, and an audit log of admin actions you take (such as lock, wipe, rename and policy changes) with a timestamp.

What we do not collect: message contents, call contents, location, browsing history, keystrokes, photos, contacts or files.

How we use it

Only to provide the Service: to show your devices in the console, deliver the commands and rules you set, keep status current, and maintain an audit trail of administrative actions. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). Devices are scoped to the account that signed in on them, enforced by security rules, so only that account can read them. Google processes this data as our infrastructure provider under its own terms and security.

Retention and deletion

Device records persist while a device is enrolled under your account. You can delete a device from the console, and wiping or un-enrolling a phone removes its management state. Audit logs are kept to provide an administrative history.

Your responsibilities and the people who use the device

If you manage a device used by someone else, you're responsible for informing them that it's managed, as required by law and by our Consent Policy. CORE MDM is not for covert monitoring.

Security

Access is authenticated, device data is scoped per account by security rules, and the in-app PIN is stored only as a salted hash. No system is perfectly secure; report issues via a security advisory.

Children

CORE MDM is operated by the adult who manages the devices. If you use it on a child's phone, you're responsible for doing so lawfully and openly.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

Provided for a beta, open-source project; not legal advice. Review your own obligations before deploying in an organization.
