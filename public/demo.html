Try the app, right here.

Things to try

The phone on the left works like the real app. Your taps stay in this page.

  1. 1
    Unlock itThe app is protected by a PIN. In the demo, any 6 digits work. Tap ✓ to go in.
  2. 2
    Open a sectionTap a card like Stop the rules being bypassed to see its switches.
  3. 3
    Flip some switchesThe count under each card's title updates as you go, just like on a real phone.
  4. 4
    Open the content filterScroll to Content Filter and tap Manage. Add a site to the block list.
  5. 5
    Switch to light modeOpen App settings and pick Light or Dark.

Demo only. On a real phone every one of these settings can also be changed from the web console, and changes reach the phone straight away.
