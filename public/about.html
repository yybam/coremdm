About

Real control, without the complexity.

CORE MDM gives families, schools and businesses the same device-owner control big companies use, set up from a browser in about a minute.

Why we built CORE MDM

Locking down an Android phone properly has always meant enterprise software, a management server and an IT department. The apps anyone could install were easy to get around — a restart into safe mode or a quick factory reset, and the rules were gone.

CORE MDM runs as device owner, the highest level of control Android gives an app. The rules you set are enforced by Android itself, not by an app the user can close. We made installing it as simple as plugging in a cable, and managing it as simple as flipping a switch in a web page.

Families

Give a child a phone with the apps and the filter you choose, and know the rules stay on.

Businesses

Keep work phones for work: block installs, lock settings, and find or wipe a lost phone.

Single-purpose devices

Turn a phone into a kiosk, a scanner or a filtered phone that runs only the apps you allow.

What we believe

Your devices are yours. Phones are tied to the account that signed in on them. No one else can see or manage them.

Control, not surveillance. CORE MDM manages what a phone can do. It doesn't read messages, track location or record browsing history. Read exactly what we collect in our Privacy Policy.

Nothing to install on your computer. The installer and the console both run in your browser.

Use it openly. CORE MDM is for devices you own or are responsible for, with the knowledge of the people who use them. It's not a tool for secretly monitoring anyone. See our Terms of Service and Consent Policy.

Open and inspectable

CORE MDM is developed in the open. The Android app, the web console and the installer are all on GitHub, with full documentation on the wiki. It's in active beta — expect steady improvements.
