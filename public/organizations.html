For organizations

One set of rules for every phone, with your name on it.

Charities, schools, communities and companies can manage a whole group of phones together: set the rules once, lock the ones that matter, and put your own logo on every lock screen.

Charities & communities

Hand out filtered phones to members and families, with the same protections on every one.

Schools

Keep students' phones for learning, with rules the school sets and parents can build on.

Companies

Give every work phone the same settings, branded with your company and managed by your team.

How it works

1

We set up your organization

CORE MDM creates your organization and makes you its owner. You can add more admins from your team.

2

Phones join with your code

You get a short join code. On each phone, open CORE MDM, choose Join an organization and type it in. The phone shows your name and asks to confirm.

3

You set the rules

Choose your rules once on the website and every phone in your organization follows them as soon as it's online.

You decide how strict each rule is

Every setting in CORE MDM can be set three ways for your whole organization.

Open

You leave it to each phone's own admin, for example a parent, to decide.

Default

Your choice applies to every phone, unless that phone's own admin changes it for their phone.

🔒 Locked

Your choice applies to every phone and nobody else can change it, not on the website and not on the phone.

Everything an organization needs

Your lock screen

Your logo, your name and your own message, such as "Property of … — if found please call …", on every phone's CORE MDM lock screen.

44 settings to lock or default

App blocks, WhatsApp and social media, parts of apps like Status and Reels, the content filter, calls, SMS, Wi-Fi, USB and more.

Several admins

The owner adds the people who help run the organization. Every admin works from the same page.

Families keep their say

Each phone still has its own admin, who manages everything you haven't locked.

Phones can't slip away

Only your organization's admins can take a phone out of the organization.

A new code any time

Make a new join code whenever you like. Phones that already joined stay in.

Questions

How do we start an organization?

Organizations are set up by the CORE MDM team, who make your organization and name its owner. The owner then signs in on the organization page to set everything up.

Do the phones need anything special?

Just CORE MDM, installed the normal way from our installer. Any phone running it can join with your code.

Can a phone's own admin change our rules?

Not the ones you lock. Settings you leave as Default they can change for their own phone, and Open settings are entirely theirs.

Can a phone leave the organization?

Only if one of your admins removes it. The person holding the phone can't take it out.

What can we see about the phones?

Each phone's name, model and when it was last online. CORE MDM manages what a phone can do. It doesn't read messages, track location or record browsing. See our Privacy Policy.
