6. Important risks: please read

CORE MDM uses Android's device-owner controls. They are powerful by design, and they are hard to reverse.

  • Setup: becoming device owner usually requires a factory-reset phone, or removing every account from it. Back up anything you need first.
  • Lock-out: rules such as blocking factory reset, safe mode, USB debugging or uninstalling can leave you unable to change or remove CORE MDM without your account. If you lose access to your account, we may not be able to help you regain control of the phone.
  • Wipe is permanent: the wipe command erases everything on the phone. It cannot be undone, and we cannot recover the data.
  • Removed apps lose their data: when "only chosen apps can install apps" is on, apps installed by any other app are uninstalled automatically, and their data is deleted with them.
  • Commands and phone behaviour: alarms, locks, reboots and restrictions affect the person using the phone. Consider their safety. For example, blocking calls may stop them phoning for help, although emergency calls are normally still allowed by Android.
  • Different brands behave differently: some manufacturers, including Samsung, add their own protections. Changing apps or settings can cause them to re-enable apps, activate Factory Reset Protection or, in rare cases, stop the phone starting up normally.
  • Content filter: no filter blocks everything. Some content may get through, and some legitimate content may be blocked.

Try new rules on one phone before using them on many. You use these features at your own risk.
