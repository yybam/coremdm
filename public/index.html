Why CORE MDM

Everything you need to keep a phone locked down

CORE MDM runs as device owner, the highest level of control Android allows an app. That means real restrictions, not settings a user can just switch back.

Remote commands

Lock the screen, sound a loud alarm to find a lost phone, reboot, or put the phone into full lockdown with one click.

App control

Block installing, uninstalling and sideloading apps. Hide the Play Store and web browsers. Turn off the camera and screenshots.

Tamper-proof

Block factory reset, safe mode and USB debugging, and protect CORE MDM from being uninstalled. The rules stay on.

Content filter

A built-in DNS filter and enforced Private DNS keep web traffic going through the filter you choose, on Wi-Fi and mobile data.

Kiosk mode

Lock the phone to only the apps you allow. Great for work phones, kids' phones, and single-purpose devices.

Hardware & network

Lock Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile network and VPN settings. Block USB file transfer, SD cards, adding users, and outgoing calls.

Live web console

See every phone with online/offline status and last seen. Changes apply the moment you flip a switch, with no refresh or re-sync.

Private to your account

Phones are tied to the account that signed in on them. Only you can see and manage your devices, and no one else's show up.

Works on any brand

Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola, Xiaomi and more. Any Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer.

FAQ

Good to know

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer, from any brand, including Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola and Xiaomi.

Why does the phone need to be reset first?

Android only lets an app become device owner on a phone with no accounts signed in. That's a safety rule so no one can quietly take over a phone that's already in use. A fresh or factory-reset phone, with Google sign-in skipped, works every time.

What do I need to install it?

Either a computer with Chrome or Edge and a USB cable, or no cable at all: factory reset the phone, tap the welcome screen 6 times and scan our setup QR code. Our web installer walks you through both. There's nothing to download.

Can the user just remove it?

No. As device owner, CORE MDM can block uninstalling, factory reset, safe mode and USB debugging, so the rules you set stay on.

Do I need internet on the phone?

The phone gets new commands and rule changes over the internet as soon as they're made. Rules you've already applied keep working even when it's offline.
