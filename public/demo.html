Things to try

The phone and the website are linked, just like the real thing: change a setting on one and watch it arrive on the other.

On the phone

  1. 1
    Unlock itThe app is protected by a PIN. In the demo, any 6 digits work. Tap ✓ to go in.
  2. 2
    Open a section and flip switchesTap a card like Stop the rules being bypassed. The count under its title updates as you go.
  3. 3
    Open the content filterScroll to Content Filter and tap Manage. Add a site to the block list.
  4. 4
    Switch to light modeOpen App settings and pick Light or Dark.

On the website

  1. 1
    Manage Sam's phoneTap Manage → on Sam's phone. It's the phone on the left.
  2. 2
    Block somethingOn the Apps tab, switch on Block social media or Block AI chats. The switch on the phone follows a moment later.
  3. 3
    Send a commandOn Commands, try Sound Alarm, Lock screen or Reboot and watch the phone.
  4. 4
    Try remote-only modeOn Device, switch on Remote-only mode. The phone's app locks, and only the website can change anything.

Demo only. Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere, and no real phone changes. On real phones, changes from the web console reach the phone straight away.
